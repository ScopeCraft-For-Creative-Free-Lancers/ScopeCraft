Help & Guide

Quick Start Workflow (Under 60 Seconds)

  1. 1. Select Deliverable & SLA: Choose your project scope, turnaround speed, and source asset volume.
  2. 2. Calibrate Regional Benchmark: Toggle your rate tier (US Baseline vs. Regional/Egypt/Global) for realistic additive market pricing without runaway compounding math.
  3. 3. Customize Terms: Click “Generate Client Agreement & SOW” to set client info, revision limits (e.g., 2 rounds), and milestone gates (50/50 split).
  4. 4. Export & Sign: Export a clean single-page contract ready for digital or wet signatures.

Legal Protection Breakdown

Intellectual Property (IP) Transfer: Explicitly retains all creative IP as contractor property until the invoice is settled 100%. Clients cannot legally publish work on partial payment.

Kill Fee & Deposits: Protects your capacity with non-refundable deposits and structured cancellation fees for killed projects.

Deemed Acceptance (The Silence Clause): Deliverables are legally approved if the client remains silent after 5 business days, preventing ghosting.

Scope Creep Surcharge: Automatically clauses out-of-scope requests at an hourly benchmark rate ($75/hr or localized equivalent).

Data Privacy & Security

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Can this PDF fit strictly on one single page?

A: Yes. The contract canvas is calibrated strictly to standard A4 dimensions with zero overflow to ensure professional presentation.

Q: What are the “Defend Your Rate” playbooks?

A: Pre-drafted objection-handling scripts designed for pushback scenarios (e.g., “The budget is too low”, “Another freelancer is cheaper”, “Can you do a free test task?”, “Net-60 terms”).

Q: What if I clear my browser cache or switch computers?

A: Pro access is tied to your browser’s local license key. Clicking “Reset License” in the footer will revert to Free mode. Keep your purchase confirmation email to re-verify across devices.
